01 A little about the work

ENGINEERING / VISUAL THINKING

Two ways of thinking.
One body of work.

I work between the logic of software and the language of visual design.

My focus is turning ideas into useful digital products, then giving those products a clear, considered visual presence. I enjoy the details on both sides: how something works, and how it feels to use.

From software projects to graphic work, this portfolio brings those parts of my practice together — without pretending they’re separate worlds.
